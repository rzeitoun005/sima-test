What's still just for show here

This is a click-through, not a working app: the Vision sliders and radar update live, but Performance Index/Capital and the life feed use seeded example numbers rather than real behavior data. Voice commands, the camera for Capture the Day, and live traffic/calendar are simulated — tapping them shows what would happen rather than actually doing it. That's the boundary between "here's the idea" and "here's the engineering," which is the next phase of work.
